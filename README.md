# Make the Judge Smile

One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened today.
A judge model compares the lines two at a time and the warmest, most surprising
line takes the $5.

Entry: `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes one fixed line to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> A kid on the bus lent me his crayon so I could finish the crossword he had been watching me fail.

97 characters, one line, UTF-8. The line was picked from 8 fresh candidates by a
pairwise tournament against the judge brief's criteria in order: a small good
thing today, warmth, surprise, one concrete detail, fresh wording.
